The following categories of repair are **explicitly flagged as Professional Only (Tier 4)** by FixerAgent. **You must not attempt these yourself:**

| Hazard | Why It Is Prohibited |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Mains voltage (>50V AC)** | Risk of electrocution, cardiac arrest, fire |
| **Microwave internal components** | Magnetron capacitors hold 2,000V+ after unplugging |
| **Gas appliances** | Gas leaks can cause explosions or carbon monoxide poisoning |
| **Refrigerant systems** | Refrigerants are pressurized and environmentally regulated; improper handling is illegal in many jurisdictions |
| **CRT monitors/TVs** | Can retain 25,000V+ lethal charge for years after unplugging |
| **Structural modifications** | May violate building codes and insurance policies |

If FixerAgent issues a 🔴 **Professional Only** classification, **stop immediately** and contact a certified technician. Ignoring this warning is at your sole risk.
